Personalized Care You Deserve

Established in 2015, MK Speciality was founded with one simple goal: to bring back the personal touch in healthcare. Unlike large hospitals, we know our patients by name and treat everyone like family.

Our Services

Comprehensive care for all ages

General Health Checkups

Annual physicals, blood pressure, cholesterol screening, diabetes checks, and preventive care.

Vaccinations & Immunizations

Flu shots, childhood vaccines, travel vaccines, tetanus, shingles, and COVID-19 vaccines.

Minor Procedures & Urgent Care

Stitches, wound care, abscess drainage, splinting, ear wax removal, and treatment for colds, flu, infections.

Women's Health

Pap smears, breast exams, contraception counseling, and menopause management.

Pediatrics & Child Care

Well-child visits, growth monitoring, developmental screening, and treatment of common childhood illnesses.

Chronic Disease Management

Diabetes, hypertension, asthma, thyroid disorders, and arthritis care with regular follow-ups.
